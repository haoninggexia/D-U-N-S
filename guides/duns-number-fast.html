How to Get a D-U-N-S Number Fast

The free official route can take 30 business days. But in most cases the delay is not D&B being slow — it is people applying for a number their company already has. Here is how to tell which situation you are in.

D-U-N-S Helper · Updated November 2026 · 6 min read

First: are you applying, or looking up?

This is the question that decides everything. Dun & Bradstreet maintains a global database of business records, and it assigns D-U-N-S Numbers to entities as part of building that database — often without the business owner ever being told. A large share of companies that "need a DUNS number" simply need to find the one they already have.

If a record exists, no application, no verification call and no 30-day wait is required. If it genuinely does not exist, D&B must create and authenticate a new record, and that process has a floor no third party can remove.

Why the free route takes so long

The form itself takes a few minutes. The wait is verification: matching the legal name against registration records, confirming the physical address, possibly calling the business, de-duplicating against existing records, then creating and propagating the new entry. Any mismatch sends you back to the start. That is why "fill the form perfectly" advice only helps so much.

Reframe the cost. If a $50,000 contract or an App Store launch is blocked, the relevant number is not the $0 fee of the free form — it is the value of three to six weeks of delay.

When a fast route does NOT apply

Be skeptical of anyone promising "instant DUNS" in every case. A genuinely fast outcome usually depends on at least one of these being true, and a reputable service will check before charging:

  • The company already has a D&B record that can be located and validated.
  • The legal name, address and registration details are consistent and verifiable.
  • The entity is a real, active legal entity (not a DBA, branch or shell).
  • The jurisdiction has adequate D&B coverage.
  • No D&B verification call or additional documentation is required for that case.

If a new record really is required, the honest answer is the D&B timeline. We state that up front rather than after payment.

A 60-second decision guide

  1. Already have a vendor portal, government filing, or past D&B email? Search there first — the number may be in an old PDF.
  2. Applying for Apple as an organization? Use Apple's lookup before D&B's general form.
  3. Brand-new company, no deadline? Submit the free D&B application and get the details exactly right.
  4. Brand-new company, hard deadline? Consider D&B's own expedited option, then plan for Apple/Google's separate verification time.
  5. Deadline this week and unsure whether a record exists? Get the existence question answered first. It is the only step that can compress weeks into minutes.

What you need ready, whichever route you take

Legal business name (exactly as registered), registered address including postcode, business phone, owner / legal representative name, legal structure, year established, primary industry and employee count. For non-English registrations, prepare a consistent English rendering — inconsistent translations are one of the most common causes of verification delays.

Avoid submitting a second application while the first is pending. Duplicate requests can freeze the case, and a D-U-N-S Number is permanent — it does not expire and is not reissued on request.

Frequently asked questions

What is the fastest legitimate way to get a D-U-N-S Number?

If your company already has a D&B record, locating and validating it is the fastest legitimate route and can take minutes. If a new record must be created, D&B's own expedited option is about 8 business days.

Is the D-U-N-S Number free?

Yes. D&B issues the standard number free of charge. Fees come from optional expedited processing or from third-party lookup and assistance services.

Can a third party really get a brand-new D-U-N-S Number instantly?

No. A new record is created and authenticated by D&B. Any service claiming to bypass that for a genuinely new entity should be treated with caution.

Does a D-U-N-S Number expire?

No. It is a permanent identifier for a business entity at a location, though D&B may mark a record inactive if the business closes or changes structure.

Is DUNS the same as a UEI or an EIN?

No. The US federal government replaced DUNS with the UEI for SAM.gov in 2022, and an EIN is a tax ID. DUNS remains the identifier used by Apple, Google Play and many international procurement processes.
