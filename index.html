What is a D-U-N-S Number?

A D-U-N-S (Data Universal Numbering System) Number is a unique nine-digit identifier issued by Dun & Bradstreet (D&B) to identify businesses worldwide. It is recognized by Apple, government agencies, and major corporations.

🔢

Unique 9-digit ID

One number that distinctly identifies your company across global databases.

🌐

Worldwide standard

Used by 300+ million businesses and required by many international programs.

🏛️

Trusted by authorities

Required for Apple Developer enrollment, U.S. government contracts, and more.

Why You Might Need One

🍎

Apple Developer

Enrolling as an organization on the Apple Developer Program requires a D-U-N-S Number.

📑

Government contracts

U.S. federal and many corporate supplier programs ask for a D-U-N-S Number.

📈

Business credit

Helps establish and verify your company's commercial identity and credit profile.

🚢

Imports & exports

Customs and international trade partners often request a D-U-N-S Number.
