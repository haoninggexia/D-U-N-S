What is a D-U-N-S Number?

A D-U-N-S (Data Universal Numbering System) Number is a unique nine-digit identifier assigned and maintained by Dun & Bradstreet (D&B) for a specific business entity at a specific location. It is used by Apple, Google, procurement teams and lenders to confirm that a company legally exists.

🔢

Unique 9-digit ID

One number identifying one legal entity at one address — not a person, not a tax ID.

🌐

Worldwide standard

Recognised across 200+ countries and required by many international programs.

🏛️

Trusted by authorities

Used for Apple Developer enrollment, supplier onboarding and tender verification.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a D-U-N-S Number free?

Yes. The standard D-U-N-S Number issued by Dun & Bradstreet is free. We charge for the lookup, verification, application assistance and support — not for the number itself.

How long does it take?

D&B's standard route takes up to 30 business days, and its paid expedited option still takes up to 8 business days. In many cases we can confirm or obtain a number within minutes. Before starting we always check your company's situation and tell you the realistic timeline for your case.

How can it be so much faster than the official process?

Most registered companies already have a D-U-N-S record in the D&B database, often created without the owner knowing it. A large part of the official wait is spent re-verifying a record that already exists. We focus on locating and validating the correct record first. If a genuinely new record is required, we tell you the real D&B timeline up front.

Do I already have one?

Many companies do. We check for an existing record before anyone applies for a new one, which also avoids duplicate applications and frozen requests.

Which countries are supported?

D-U-N-S Numbers are issued globally. Send us your company name and country and we will confirm availability and timing for your region.

Are you affiliated with Dun & Bradstreet or Apple?

No. We are an independent third-party assistance service. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of Dun & Bradstreet, Apple, or Google. The number is assigned and maintained by D&B.
